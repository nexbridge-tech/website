## Precision parts, scoped around your drawing

NexBridge supports international OEMs and engineering teams sourcing custom mechanical parts in Vietnam. We review your drawings and coordinate CNC machining with manufacturers, connecting technical questions, sample approval and delivery requirements through one point of contact.

Bring a released drawing package or an early model that needs manufacturability feedback. The production route, material, achievable tolerances and inspection coverage are confirmed against the specific part before quotation approval.

## CNC milling, turning and mechanical components

- **Milled parts:** electronics housings, mounting plates, brackets, covers and custom structural components.
- **Turned parts:** shafts, spacers, bushings and other rotational components, subject to geometry and material review.
- **Mechanical assemblies:** coordinate mating parts, purchased hardware and assembly requirements within an agreed work package.
- **Prototype and repeat builds:** define initial quantities, first-part review and the approved revision for subsequent orders.

Material requests may include aluminum alloys, steel, stainless steel and engineering plastics. Specify the exact grade, finish and any required material evidence. Availability and process suitability are checked with the selected manufacturer.

## Review what affects fit, function and cost

We clarify datums, mating interfaces, threads, surface finish, edge conditions and critical dimensions before release. Where geometry or a tolerance creates a manufacturing constraint, proposed changes are returned for your approval.

Inspection requirements should identify the characteristics to measure, the reporting format and any first-article or sampling expectations. Certification, traceability and special-process requirements must be specified in the RFQ and confirmed in the agreed scope.

## Connect mechanical parts with PCBA and tooling

For electronic products, review enclosure mounting points, connector access and assembly clearances alongside the [PCB design and PCBA package](/solutions/pcb-design-pcba-vietnam.html). For repeat assembly or inspection, include [custom jigs and fixtures](/solutions/jigs-fixtures-vietnam.html) in the same technical review.

## What your team receives

Depending on the quotation, deliverables can include manufactured parts, agreed dimensional inspection results, material documentation, approved deviation records and packaging identification linked to the drawing revision. Shipping arrangements and delivery responsibilities are agreed before order acceptance.

## Prepare a machining RFQ

1. STEP or agreed 3D CAD files and dimensioned PDF drawings with revision identifiers.
2. Material grade, surface treatment, threads, critical tolerances and inspection requirements.
3. Prototype quantity, expected repeat volumes and target schedule.
4. Delivery country, packaging needs and any confidentiality requirements.
